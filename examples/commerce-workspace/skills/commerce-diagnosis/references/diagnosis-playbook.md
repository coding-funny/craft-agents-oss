# Diagnosis playbook

1. Detect sales, order, unit, and refund changes.
2. Check inventory snapshots and coverage.
3. Check promotion sponsor, stacking, and contribution economics.
4. Check advertising spend, CTR, CVR, CPC, ROAS, and attribution alignment.
5. Compare support, counter-evidence, and missing data before assigning confidence.

- Spend up and conversion down: investigate paid-traffic efficiency without claiming it alone caused total sales decline.
- Clicks up with constrained stock: retain both facts and prioritize availability as a limiting hypothesis.
- Ads stable while sales falls: request organic-traffic evidence.
- Attribution missing: mark the advertising branch evidence-insufficient.

All recommendations are review inputs, not execution authority.
