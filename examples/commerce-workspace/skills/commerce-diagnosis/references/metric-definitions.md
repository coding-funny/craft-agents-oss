# Metric definitions

- Windows are half-open `[start, end)` and comparisons use equal duration and timezone.
- Monetary values are integer minor units and currencies are never combined.
- Net sales = paid GMV - refunds in the selected window.
- Contribution = net sales - product cost - platform fee - fulfillment cost.
- CTR = clicks / impressions; CVR = attributed orders / clicks; CPC = spend / clicks; ROAS = attributed revenue / spend.
- Missing denominators and attribution records are unknown, not zero.
- Derived metrics inherit the evidence IDs of their source records.
